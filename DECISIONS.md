# Decisions

## One event, not a permanent team profile

The retained pain is stale recruitment and uncertain remaining capacity. Every listing therefore represents one future event and expires after it. Permanent team pages, followers, reviews, and historical feeds are outside the pilot.

## Anonymous applicant status

Applicants receive an unguessable status URL. Organizers can accept, waitlist, or decline without collecting names, email, phone numbers, or public account links. Accepted applicants see the private join instructions.

## No payment or identity layer

Participation fees are displayed but settled at the venue. Better Auth, payment processing, age collection, identity verification, and public direct messages are not included.

## Discovery

The live directory contains current, non-hidden future listings. Individual listings are noindex during the pilot; the directory, guide, and privacy pages are indexed. Acquisition is limited to search, Tool Shelf, the public repository, and voluntary sharing.
