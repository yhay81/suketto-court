# Public pilot

## Product promise

Publish one fresh futsal vacancy, receive a minimal anonymous application, and give accepted helpers the participation instructions without exposing contact details.

## Signals

- real visitors
- organizers creating listings
- applicants submitting to a listing
- listings receiving at least one real application
- accepted applications
- listings whose required slots are filled
- returning browsers

Automated QA does not count.

## Initial decision rule

Review after at least 30 days. Continue product investment when at least five real organizers publish, twenty real applicants apply, three listings receive applications, and two listings accept a helper. Hold if fewer than two listings receive an application or no helper is accepted.
